Crypto Market Analysis Report

1. Introduction
The cryptocurrency market is highly volatile, and real-time data is crucial for traders and analysts. This report summarizes insights from live cryptocurrency data fetched from the CoinGecko API and updated every 5 minutes in an Excel file.

2. Data Insights
Top Cryptocurrencies: The dataset includes top 50 cryptocurrencies based on market capitalization.
Price Trends: Bitcoin (BTC) and Ethereum (ETH) remain dominant, with significant fluctuations in 24-hour price change percentages.
Market Capitalization: The highest market cap belongs to Bitcoin, followed by Ethereum and Binance Coin.
Trading Volume:High trading volumes indicate strong liquidity, with Bitcoin and Ethereum leading in daily transactions.
24-Hour Price Change: Volatility analysis shows that certain altcoins have over 10% price changes within 24 hours, indicating rapid market shifts.

3. Conclusion & Future Scope
The data demonstrates the dynamic nature of the crypto market. This live-updating Excel sheet can be used for:
Tracking price fluctuations for investment decisions
Identifying high-volatility coins for day trading strategies
Developing predictive models using historical data trends
